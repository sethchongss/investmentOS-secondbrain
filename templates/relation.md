`relation` is one of: supports, contradicts, extends, supersedes, duplicates.

`from` is the record you are judging. `to` is the record it acts on. For `supersedes` and `duplicates`, `from` is the newer capture and `to` is the one you keep as canonical.

The note should say what, specifically, is supported or contradicted. A record can hold a superseded primary claim and a side comment that is only contested. Say which.
