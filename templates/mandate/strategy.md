One strategy per file. Copy this to `vault/profile/strategies/<id>.md` and keep only the fields for that `strategy_kind`. Delete this guidance after the fields are filled, and keep the file fictional-label free of client names.

PE fields, and no others: `ownership`, `deal_type`, `value_creation`, `earnings_quality`, `cash_debt`, `downside`.

VC fields, and no others: `stage`, `ownership`, `follow_on`, `unit_economics`.

Hedge-fund fields, and no others: `hf_style`, `holding_period`, `exposure`, `liquidity`, `catalysts`, `risk`.

Fund-selection fields, and no others: `gp_strategy`, `gp_team`, `attribution`, `terms`, `repeatability`.

Do not put buyout fields on a venture file, or GP terms on a control-deal file. A second strategy gets a second file.
