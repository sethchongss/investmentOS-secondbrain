# An attach rate needs a denominator

Developing only. `evidence_for` has one library record, below the three-record bar this vault uses for a `recurring` principle. Do not cite this page as a settled rule.

## Evidence for

- [lib-lom-002](../../library/lib-lom-002.md), Mullwick Procurement Digest, posted 3 Feb 2026. Three named awards, one with Brack Flags. The source says it is not a shipment sample.

## Evidence against

- [lib-lom-001](../../library/lib-lom-001.md), posted 14 Jan 2026, says Brack Flags rode on about 40% of shipments. No denominator or sample is in the capture.

## What would make this recurring

Two further sourced cases where a stated attach rate and a named-award list disagree, or one source that both states a rate and shows the denominator. Until then, keep the disagreement on the company page as contested, not as a new number.
