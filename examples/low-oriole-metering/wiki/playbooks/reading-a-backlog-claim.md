# Reading a backlog claim

Use this when a note, a filing, or a transcript gives a backlog, a remaining-performance figure, or a "contracted" total. The Low Oriole pages are the illustration. The steps are the part worth keeping when you file a real issuer in your private vault.

1. Write the noun the source used. "Contracted backlog" and "core backlog" were different objects in the example. Keep the noun in the claim sentence.
2. Look for three disclosures before you reuse a growth rate: options included or not, cancellations netted or not, conversion timing. The rule is [prin-backlog-definition](../principles/backlog-definition.md).
3. Search `link` and `post_id` before you add a record. The validator rejects a second file with the same value. A repeat under a new link still needs a `duplicates` relation, as with the 12 Mar clip.
4. Put the claim in your own words in the library record. Leave the excerpt in `raw/`. Do not edit the old claim sentence when a better definition arrives. Add a record and a `supersedes` relation, then propose the wiki change.
5. In the memo, cite the saved record and the date posted. Put comparison and judgment under Inference.

This playbook does not say which accounting definition a real company should use. The glossary entry on contracted backlog says the same limit.
