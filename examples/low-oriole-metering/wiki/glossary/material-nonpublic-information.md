# Material nonpublic information

Do not capture it here. Do not paste it into a prompt. Do not "anonymize" it into a source stub.

This glossary defines the fence. It does not contain an example of such information. If a note would matter to a reasonable investor and is not already public, it does not belong in `raw/`, `library/`, the wiki, or a memo.

Public material can still be copyrighted. Keep excerpts short, keep them in your private vault, and do not publish someone else's article in full. The synthetic excerpts in this example were written for the starter.
