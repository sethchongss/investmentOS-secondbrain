# Attach rate

A rate needs a numerator, a denominator, and a period. "About 40% of shipments" without the shipment count is not yet a rate you can reuse. "One of three named awards" is a list, not a rate, and it can still contradict the slogan.

The example keeps both and calls the disagreement contested. See [prin-attach-denominator](../principles/attach-needs-a-denominator.md).
