Synthetic primer written for this starter. It is not a textbook, and it cites no real accounting standard.

A backlog dollar figure is comparable only when the note states three things: whether unexercised options are inside the number, whether cancellations are netted, and how long conversion is expected to take. If any of the three is missing, do not compute a growth rate on that figure and then carry the rate onto a later figure that uses a different definition. The author's confidence does not fill in a missing definition.
