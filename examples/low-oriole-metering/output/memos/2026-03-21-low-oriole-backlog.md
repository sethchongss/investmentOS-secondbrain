# Low Oriole backlog after the March restatement

Research note on a fictional issuer. Not investment advice. Not a recommendation. No return is implied. Every figure below is synthetic and lives in a saved record.

## Sources

- On 2 Nov 2025 the comparability primer said a backlog figure is not comparable until options, cancellations, and conversion timing are explicit. [lib-primer-001](../../library/lib-primer-001.md).
- On 14 Jan 2026 Lantern Desk Notes reported contracted backlog of $180 million including unexercised options, growth of 22% year on year, conversion within two quarters, Brack Flags on about 40% of shipments, and FY2025 revenue of $246 million. Captured 15 Jan 2026. [lib-lom-001](../../library/lib-lom-001.md).
- On 3 Feb 2026 the Mullwick digest listed three meter awards. Only Mullwick took Brack Flags. The digest said this is not a shipment sample. [lib-lom-002](../../library/lib-lom-002.md).
- On 11 Mar 2026 the Port Hinge Ledger reported core backlog of $112 million, an options bucket of $68 million, and firm-order conversion of about 14 months. It said the two buckets together are the January $180 million, and that the $68 million is options rather than cancellations. [lib-lom-003](../../library/lib-lom-003.md).
- On 12 Mar 2026 a Lantern Desk clip repeated that ledger item under a different link. Treated as a duplicate, not as confirmation. [lib-lom-005](../../library/lib-lom-005.md).
- On 18 Mar 2026 a synthetic Q&A with the fictional CFO confirmed the $112 million core definition and did not restate an attach rate. [lib-lom-004](../../library/lib-lom-004.md).

## Inference

The move from $180 million to $112 million is a change of definition, not a sourced loss of demand. The composition is already stated by the ledger and confirmed by the Q&A, so that sentence is not an extra guess. The judgment I am adding is this: the 22% growth rate should not be repeated, because its base was the superseded total.

The 40% attach sentence and the 1-of-3 award list disagree. I do not have a replacement rate. Revenue of $246 million is still one source. I am not using it.

Nothing here is a valuation, a target, or a view on a real security.
