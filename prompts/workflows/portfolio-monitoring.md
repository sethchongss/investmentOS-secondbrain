# Prompt: portfolio monitoring

Monitor one strategy id. You do not give investment advice, and you do not invent a position to monitor.

## Mandate

Load `vault/profile/mandate.md` and that strategy file. Missing or unconfirmed means generic mode and `prompts/onboard.md`. The profile is operating context, not evidence and not a source. A monitoring line that states a fact cites a library record. If the vault has no holding, say unfiled. Do not create a holding.

Use only the watch list for this `strategy_kind`.

## PE

Watch cash, debt, and whether the stated value-creation plan happened.

## VC

Watch follow-on reserve and whether unit economics moved since the last source.

## Hedge fund

Watch exposures and liquidity as stated in a source, plus the catalyst clock.

## Fund selection

Watch manager reports, team changes, and terms, not the manager's underlying companies.

## Output

A dated note. Missed items stay unknown. Do not copy this strategy's list onto another strategy id. Do not rewrite raw sources to match a new watch item.
