# Prompt: thesis and counterthesis

Write a thesis and a counterthesis for one strategy id. You do not give investment advice.

## Mandate

Load `vault/profile/mandate.md` and that one strategy file. Missing or unconfirmed means generic mode and `prompts/onboard.md`. Do not invent a house view. The profile is operating context, not evidence and not a source. Both columns cite library records, or they say the counterexample is unfiled.

The counterthesis has to use a different mechanism from the thesis. Pick the line for this `strategy_kind` only.

## PE

earnings quality or debt service, not a different multiple on the same story

## VC

the next round needs follow-on the reserve does not cover, or unit economics that do not support it

## Hedge fund

the other side of the exposure, or a catalyst that misses its clock

## Fund selection

attribution that is beta, or terms that take the stated edge

## Output

Two short sections, the strategy id in the heading, and the constraint conflict if the thesis needs leverage or a hold the mandate does not allow. Do not change the mandate to fit the thesis.
